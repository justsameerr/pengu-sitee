Privacy

Plain language, no tricks. Last updated for the current version of the bot.

What the bot stores

  • Your Discord user ID.
  • Your game data: cowoncy and bank balance, animals, team, gems, weapons, level and XP, daily streak, quest and achievement progress, expeditions, titles and badges.
  • Who you are married to (their user ID), counters for gifs and emotes you send, and pet nicknames you choose.
  • A wallpaper image, only if you upload one with the wallpaper command.
  • Per-server settings an admin chooses: the prefix, disabled commands and family-friendly mode.
  • Suggestions you send with the suggest command.

What the bot reads

The bot needs the Message Content permission to notice commands that start with its prefix. It also gives a small amount of XP for chatting. It does not store the text of your messages, except text you deliberately give it to keep, such as a suggestion or a pet nickname.

What the website shows

The leaderboard and player pages show your Discord username, avatar, level, title, badges and game stats if you are among the top players. The data is refreshed on a schedule, so changes appear after the next update. You can hide yourself at any time with pengu privacy web off, and your page disappears at the next update.

Other services

Some commands send a request to a public service: gifs for emotes and actions come from nekos.best and waifu.pics, meme images from memegen.link, definitions from dictionaryapi.dev, translations from MyMemory, and emoji pictures from public content networks. Only what the command needs (such as the text of a meme) is sent. This site loads your Discord avatar from Discord's own servers.

Removing your data

To have your game data deleted, ask the bot owner in the support server or in the server where you use the bot. Removing the bot from a server stops it reading messages there, but your game data stays until it is deleted.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). The bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
